Taskclu · iOS and Android · com.taskclu.taskclu

Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

This policy describes the Taskclu app. Taskclu is for people who run a company or a project. It has no account and no server of its own.

This is the public copy of the policy shown inside the app.

Your data stays on this device

Projects, assignments, tasks, people, meetings, notes, recordings, expenses, decisions, photos, files and reports are stored on this phone or tablet. Names, roles, phone numbers and email addresses you add for people stay here too. Taskclu does not receive them.

What Taskclu does not do

Taskclu does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or usage data. It does not sell data, and it does not use your work to train models. There is no Taskclu account to sign in to.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

Camera and photos — to scan a document, take a picture, or attach a photo you choose.

Microphone — to record a meeting when you tap Record. The recording stays on this device. Taskclu does not transcribe it.

Notifications — for the morning brief, and only if you turn it on. The notice is written on this device from your own tasks and meetings.

Face or fingerprint unlock — to lock Taskclu. The phone checks that it is you. Taskclu never receives your face or fingerprint.

Backups

A backup is a file you create and save where you choose, such as iCloud, Google Drive, or a folder on this device. The file contains your data and the photos, recordings and other files stored in the app. Taskclu does not upload it. Anyone who can open that file can read it.

When you share or send a message

Sharing a report, sending a backup, or nudging someone opens another app — mail, messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, or the share sheet — with the text or file you chose. From there, that app handles it under its own policy. Taskclu does not send it.

Writing to Taskclu

Contact, ideas and problem reports open your email app. A problem report includes the Taskclu version and the kind of device, so the message is easier to understand. Taskclu receives only what you choose to send to eeshbaev@outlook.com.

Ratings

Rate Taskclu opens your app store’s page. Apple or Google handle that visit under their own policy. Taskclu does not receive your rating.

Children

Taskclu is not directed at children under 13, or under the age required where you live.

Deleting your data

Erase everything, in Settings, deletes Taskclu’s data on this device and returns you to the start. Uninstalling the app removes what the system deletes with it. A backup you already saved somewhere else stays until you delete that file.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. The new wording is the one shown here.

Contact

Questions about privacy: eeshbaev@outlook.com
