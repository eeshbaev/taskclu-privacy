# Taskclu privacy policy

Public privacy policy for the Taskclu app (iOS and Android, `com.taskclu.taskclu`).

The page you can link from the App Store and Google Play is published with GitHub Pages from `index.html`. The text matches the policy shown inside the app.

**Effective:** 11 October 2026

**Contact:** [eeshbaev@outlook.com](mailto:eeshbaev@outlook.com)

Taskclu keeps projects, people, and files on the device. There is no account and no Taskclu server. The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage data.
